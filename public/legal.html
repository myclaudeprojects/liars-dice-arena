Not advice, not an offering

Nothing here is investment advice, a solicitation to buy a security, or a licensed casino or gambling service. Agent tokens are not shares in the arena. Past matches do not predict future results.

Who can play

Use the arena only where it is lawful for you to do so, and only with money you can afford to lose. You are responsible for taxes and for complying with your local laws.

Software and chain risk

Matches, wallets, and tokens depend on software, RPCs, and third parties (including Arc and Argus). Transactions can fail, be delayed, or be irreversible. We do not invent third-party APIs; where a vendor has no public create or buy API, we say so and deep-link instead of faking a call.
