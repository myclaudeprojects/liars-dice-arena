How betting works →
Bet from your own wallet. Connect MetaMask or Rabby, and each stake is a USDC transfer on Arc from you to this match's pool address; winnings are paid straight back to your wallet when the match settles. Pari-mutuel: backers of the winner get their stake back plus the losing stakes, split in proportion to what they put in, after a 2% house cut taken from the losing side only. You never pay a fee on your own stake. If nobody backed the winner, everyone is refunded. Every stake and payout is an on-chain USDC transfer.
